# GardenOS productivity fallback

Every canonical crop has a nonzero planning estimate in `productivity-assumptions.json`. **This is a planning aid, not a horticultural claim of verified output.** The data intentionally separates sourced RHS mature-plant yields from provisional assumptions. Do not copy provisional values into RHS field assertions or label them verified.

Use the tiers in order: user-calibrated > evidence-backed > provisional. Multiply only when the planting-count unit matches the yield basis; otherwise return an advisory and ask for a conversion. In particular, seed tubers, crowns, trees, bushes, cobs and individual plants are not interchangeable. For permanent crops, account for plant establishment before applying mature-plant estimates. Uncertainty should propagate to the household yield forecast and optimiser.

The provisional figures are broad heuristic starting ranges, deliberately conservative in interpretation, **not measured RHS trial data**. Courgette, cucumber, tomato and beans depend strongly on variety, growing method and season. Basil and herb weights depend on repeated picking. Crop loss and crop failure are not captured by the ranges; forecasts should include a separate success factor. Historical user harvest records should be used to calibrate these estimates over time.
